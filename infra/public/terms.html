LastCall — Terms of use

By creating an account or sending content you agree to these terms, the Community guidelines and the Privacy policy.

Your account

You must be at least 18 years old. Your device key identifies your account. If you lose it, a confirmed email address lets you restore your username on a new key, and your contacts are warned that the key changed. Chat history, contacts and groups are not restored, and an account with no confirmed address cannot be restored at all. Keep your mailbox secure: whoever controls it can restore your account. Do not share your device or key. One person may not impersonate another.

Content and conduct

You are responsible for the messages, files, voice notes, video circles and calls you send. Do not send or request content that is illegal, sexually exploits minors, promotes violence or hatred, harasses another person, infringes rights, contains malware or reveals another person’s private data without consent. Do not use the service for spam or fraud.

Moderation

You can block users and report content or accounts from a chat’s safety menu. Reports are sent to the LastCall moderation queue with the reported account or group, your authenticated account, the reason and optional details. We review reports, may restrict or remove accounts and retain report records for moderation and legal obligations. Because message content is not stored on the server, we may be unable to review messages unless you provide them. We may suspend access to protect users or comply with the law.

Service and liability

The service is provided “as is” without warranties. It has not passed an independent security audit, does not implement Double Ratchet, and must not be used for data requiring certified protection. To the extent permitted by law, the operator is not liable for lost keys, lost local data, delivery delays, network costs or content sent by users. Availability may change; the service may be discontinued.

Contact

Questions, appeals and moderation requests: admin@lienad.ru.
